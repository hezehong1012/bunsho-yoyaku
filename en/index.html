Frequently asked questions

Is this really free?

Yes. There is no trial clock, no card and no account. A single IP gets 60 runs per day, which is enough for normal reading work.

Do you store or train on my text?

No. Your text is used to produce the summary and nothing else. Results are cached in memory for up to six hours so a repeat request is instant, then dropped.

How accurate is it?

It keeps facts and figures well, but it can still miss a nuance. For anything you will publish, file or send to a client, read the source once before sending the summary.
