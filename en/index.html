Why these exist

The reading tax is real: forty minutes to find the three paragraphs that mattered in a report, twenty minutes to make a transcript look like notes, thirty minutes to write an email that takes three minutes to read. These tools do the first pass. You still make the judgement — that part is not outsourced.Each tool has one job and a fixed prompt written natively in English, for a US or European reader: conclusion first, numbers kept, hedging cut, no translated-Japanese phrasing.

FAQ

Is everything here free?

Yes — all nine tools, no trial clock, no card, no account. One IP gets 60 runs per day.

Do you store or train on what I paste?

No. Text is used to produce the result, cached in memory for up to six hours, then dropped. Nothing is stored long-term.

US or UK English?

Default output is US business English. The proofreader follows the spelling you already use, so a UK draft stays UK.
