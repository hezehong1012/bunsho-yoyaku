About AI Summary

AI Summary is a small, independent project. It provides free browser-based writing tools — summaries, paraphrasing, proofreading, email drafts, weekly reports, speeches, meeting notes and blog drafts — for people who work in English and do not want another subscription.

The tools run on a lightweight LLM backend with prompts written natively in English, not translated from another language. Nothing requires an account, a trial period or a card. Each IP gets a daily allowance that resets at midnight; the point is to keep the service usable rather than to harvest signups.

How it is funded

Out of pocket, for now. If usage grows past what the current setup can carry, a paid tier with higher limits will be added — the free tools stay free.
