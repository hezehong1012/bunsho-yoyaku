FAQ

How do I get the transcript out of Zoom?

Recording → Audio transcript → download the .vtt or .txt. Teams keeps it in the meeting chat; Meet writes it to the host's Drive. Your own notes work too.

Will it attribute the right person?

Only when the transcript is explicit. Named, diarised transcripts work well; “Speaker 2” stays “Speaker 2”. Check names before sending.

Does it invent decisions?

It can promote a strong suggestion into a decision when the transcript is ambiguous. Delete anything that was not actually agreed — these are draft notes, not the minutes.
