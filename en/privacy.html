Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The text you paste

Your text is sent to the model provider that powers the tools, solely to produce the result you asked for. It is not stored in a database, not attached to a profile, and not used to train models.

Results are cached in server memory for up to six hours so an identical repeat request returns instantly. That cache is in memory only, expires on its own, and is keyed to the request, not to a person.

What is logged

Standard request metadata: timestamp, path, response status, and a hashed IP address used for rate limiting. IP addresses are used only to count requests per day; we do not build behavioural profiles and we do not sell or share this data.

Cookies and analytics

No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, no fingerprinting. A small browser-side storage entry may be used to keep your last-used settings on this device; you can clear it at any time.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Vercel and model inference is provided by an external LLM API. Both process data as processors, under their own terms, only to deliver the request.

Your rights

Because nothing is stored, there is usually nothing to delete or export. If you are in the EU or UK and want to exercise a GDPR or UK GDPR right, email hezehong1012@outlook.com and we will answer within the statutory period.

Do not paste secrets

These are public tools. Do not paste credentials, medical records, or anything you are contractually forbidden to send to a third-party processor.
