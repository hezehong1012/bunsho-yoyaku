How long documents are handled

Inputs above roughly 8,000 characters are split into sections. Each section is condensed into notes that keep names, dates, figures and conclusions, and those notes are then merged into one summary. That is why a 15,000-character report comes back as a single piece of prose instead of four stitched-together fragments.

For documents over the limit, split at natural boundaries — chapter, section, agenda item — and summarize each, then summarize the summaries.

Frequently asked questions

How long a document can it take?

Up to 15,000 characters in one run, roughly 2,500 words. Longer documents are split into sections internally and merged. A PDF's text can be pasted in the same way.

Will it invent facts?

It is instructed never to add facts, and in practice it stays close to the source. It can still compress two similar statements into one and lose a caveat, so check anything you will act on.

Can I keep specific figures or names?

Yes — put them in the 'Must include' field. It is the most reliable way to stop a number or a product name from being dropped.

Is my text kept?

No. It is processed to produce the summary and cached in memory for at most six hours so repeat runs are instant. Nothing is stored long-term and nothing is used for training.

Does it work for non-English sources?

It will summarize them, but the output is English. Quality is highest when the source is already English.
