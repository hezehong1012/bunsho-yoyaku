What “plainer” actually does

It replaces nominalisations with verbs, splits sentences that carry three ideas, and cuts the qualifiers that make a commitment sound like a hedge. “The utilisation of the revised process resulted in a reduction of processing time” becomes “Using the new process cut processing time roughly in half.” Same fact, 40% fewer words.

Use it as an editor, not a laundromat

Paraphrasing your own draft is ordinary editing. Paraphrasing someone else's paragraph to hide that it was theirs is plagiarism with extra steps — the ordering of ideas survives rewriting, and that is what gets noticed.

FAQ

Will it change my meaning?

It is told to keep every fact, number and name, but nuance can slip — a “may” turning into a “will”. Reread it if you will be held to the wording.

Is this safe for academic writing?

As editing, yes. As a way to disguise copied text, no — that is misconduct, and detectors read structure, not just vocabulary.

Can it match my voice?

Pick the closest mode and put one sentence of your own in the same box. Short style cues propagate well.
