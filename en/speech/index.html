What makes a draft usable

One concrete moment beats three adjectives. “She once drove back forty miles because a client left a jacket in the conference room” tells an audience more than “she is incredibly dedicated”. The tool is prompted to build around the detail you supply, so supply one.

Then read it aloud once, standing up. Anything you stumble on, cut. A speech is finished when it is easier to say than to read.

Timing rules of thumb

130 words per minute at a relaxed pace, 150 if you are rushing because you are nervous. Wedding toasts: 90 seconds is generous. Retirement send-offs: two minutes, with one laugh and one sincere line. Keynotes: add 20% to whatever you timed in rehearsal.

FAQ

How long is a 2-minute speech really?

About 260–300 words — roughly one double-spaced page read aloud. Toasts should be shorter than people plan.

Will the jokes land?

Mild humour lands; wordplay often does not when delivered nervously. Test each line on one person and cut anything that needs explaining.

Does it know about the person?

Only what you give it. Two specific details are the difference between a draft that sounds like them and one that sounds like a template.
