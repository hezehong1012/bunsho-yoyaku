What it fixes and what it leaves alone

Fixed: subject-verb agreement, verb tense and form, articles, prepositions, spelling, capitalisation, punctuation, run-on sentences, doubled words, and inconsistent spelling such as mixing “organize” with “organise” in one document.

Left alone: your voice, sentence length, and any deliberate fragment. A cover letter that sounds like you is worth more than one that sounds like a grammar textbook, so the tool does not flatten style.

Where people use it

Non-native English speakers sending an email to a US client; a resume where one wrong “their” costs an interview; an internal doc that will be read by forty people; a thesis abstract before submission.

FAQ

Does it rewrite my sentences?

No. It fixes errors and leaves your phrasing alone. If you want a different tone or a shorter sentence, use the paraphraser.

Will it catch tricky grammar?

Standard usage is reliable, including “if I were” and comma splices. It can over-correct deliberate fragments, so read the change list before accepting it.

Is this a plagiarism checker?

No. It does not compare your text against a corpus. Corrected text is still your text.
